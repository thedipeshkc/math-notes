Chapter 3: Factoring

Factoring is the reverse of multiplication — we rewrite a polynomial as a product of simpler polynomials. It is one of the most important algebraic skills, used constantly in solving equations, simplifying fractions, and calculus.

3.1 — Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

Always check for a GCF first before applying any other technique.

Worked Example 3.1

Factor: $6x^4 - 9x^3 + 15x^2$

Solution: GCF is $3x^2$:

$$6x^4 - 9x^3 + 15x^2 = 3x^2(2x^2 - 3x + 5)$$

3.2 — Factoring Trinomials

Factoring $x^2 + bx + c$
Find two numbers $p$ and $q$ such that $p + q = b$ and $p \cdot q = c$. Then: $$x^2 + bx + c = (x+p)(x+q)$$
Factoring $ax^2 + bx + c$ (AC Method)
Compute $ac$. Find factors $p, q$ of $ac$ with $p+q=b$. Split the middle term and factor by grouping.

Worked Example 3.2

Factor: $6x^2 + 7x - 3$

Solution: $a=6, b=7, c=-3$, so $ac = -18$. We need $p+q=7$ and $pq=-18$: choose $p=9, q=-2$.

$$6x^2 + 9x - 2x - 3 = 3x(2x+3) - 1(2x+3) = (3x-1)(2x+3)$$

3.3 — Special Factoring Patterns

Patterns to Memorize
$$\begin{align} a^2 - b^2 &= (a+b)(a-b) \quad \text{(difference of squares)} \\ a^2 + 2ab + b^2 &= (a+b)^2 \quad \text{(perfect square trinomial)} \\ a^2 - 2ab + b^2 &= (a-b)^2 \quad \text{(perfect square trinomial)} \\ a^3 - b^3 &= (a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2) \quad \text{(difference of cubes)} \\ a^3 + b^3 &= (a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2) \quad \text{(sum of cubes)} \end{align}$$
Warning — Sum of Squares
$a^2 + b^2$ does not factor over the real numbers. There is no real factoring of $x^2 + 9$, for example.

Worked Example 3.3

Factor completely: $3x^5 - 48x$

Solution: Factor out the GCF first, then apply difference of squares twice:

$$3x^5 - 48x = 3x(x^4 - 16) = 3x(x^2+4)(x^2-4) = 3x(x^2+4)(x+2)(x-2)$$

3.4 — Factoring by Grouping

For four-term polynomials, group in pairs and factor each group.

Worked Example 3.4

Factor: $x^3 + 2x^2 - 9x - 18$

$$= x^2(x+2) - 9(x+2) = (x^2-9)(x+2) = (x+3)(x-3)(x+2)$$

Practice Problems

Solutions:
Problem 1
Factor completely: $12x^3y^2 - 8x^2y^3 + 4xy^4$
Solution

GCF is $4xy^2$:

$$4xy^2(3x^2 - 2xy + y^2)$$

The trinomial $3x^2 - 2xy + y^2$ does not factor further (discriminant $< 0$).

Problem 2
Factor: $2x^2 - 5x - 12$
Solution

$ac = -24$. Need $p+q = -5$, $pq = -24$: choose $p = 3, q = -8$.

$$2x^2 + 3x - 8x - 12 = x(2x+3) - 4(2x+3) = (x-4)(2x+3)$$
Problem 3
Factor completely: $x^4 - 81$
Solution
$$x^4 - 81 = (x^2)^2 - 9^2 = (x^2+9)(x^2-9) = (x^2+9)(x+3)(x-3)$$

Note: $x^2+9$ does not factor over the reals.

Problem 4
Factor: $8x^3 + 27$
Solution

Sum of cubes with $a = 2x$, $b = 3$:

$$8x^3+27 = (2x)^3+3^3 = (2x+3)(4x^2-6x+9)$$
Problem 5
Factor completely: $2x^4 + x^3 - 18x^2 - 9x$
Solution
$$= x(2x^3 + x^2 - 18x - 9) = x\bigl[x^2(2x+1) - 9(2x+1)\bigr] = x(x^2-9)(2x+1)$$ $$= x(x+3)(x-3)(2x+1)$$
